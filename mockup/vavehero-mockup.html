A rhythm game for the M-VAVE FM-1 · concept mockup
A Dinghy 0.5 app, the same family as RoboSeq and Star DistroyerNot an official M-VAVE product
Key LED lit: press now Glow: the next note The play window on the screen, one octave
The screen at full resolution, 240 × 240 px, with Dinghy's own Terminus fonts (16 and 32 px). Enlarged 2×.

    Song select

    SELECT scrolls, KNOB 1 picks the level, PLAY starts. Medals and the best percentage are kept in flash.

    OP BOOST

    Six operators full and FX pressed: the highway glows, the score doubles, and the sound opens up with more modulation.

    Results

    Grade, hits and timing. The average offset fine-tunes the latency calibration.

    Controls during play

    WHITE KEYSPlay the notes. The LED shows what is coming.
    FXSet off OP BOOST when the meter is full.
    OCT− / OCT+Move the play window an octave when the song asks for it.
    PLAYPause and go on. Blinks in time.
    KNOB 1SWEEP: turn into the target arc in sweep parts.
    HOMEBack to song select.
    KNOB 2TONE: the FM sound of your voice.
    SELECTScroll songs and menus.
    KNOB 3SPEED: practice tempo, 50–100 %.
    RECRecord your own song as a new note chart.
    KNOB 4MIX: your voice against the backing.
    MASTERVolume.
    The backing (drums, bass, chords) and your voice are played by the app's own engine, as in RoboSeq and SkurkyMello. Only the white keys are played. The black ones are shown but do nothing. With SPREAD, every other white key is used, for big fingers. The LEDs are white and have two levels (fm1_led_key and fm1_led_key_glow), so colour exists only on the screen.
