You can also use the panel: click or touch the keys and buttons, and turn a knob by dragging it (up or right turns it up), with the mouse wheel, or by tapping its left or right half. The knobs say what they do now, as the screen's knob row does. The LEDs show what they would on an FM-1: lit means press now, a glow means the next note.

Settings

How dirty the sound is: pumping from the kick, saturation on the mix and crunch on the drums. 0 is clean.

MIDI in

Channel 1 plays the keys: an FM-1 with firmware that sends them over USB-MIDI, or an ordinary MIDI keyboard. With MIDI the game shifts octaves by itself. Channels 2–6 and 10 play the band's instruments, so you can hear a song from your DAW through the audio engine while you make it.

MIDI MODE (the last row of the song list, or M): LIVE is a sound module right away. PREVIEW delays everything by one bar, so channel 1 falls down the highway as in the game. The keys go out on channel 1 to “Out”, so your DAW can record them. See docs/songs.md.

Your own songs (MIDI)

Channel 1 is the play track. 2 BASS, 3 PAD, 4 ARP, 5 LEAD, 6 KEYS and 10 drums (General MIDI). Channel 16 is reserved for LINK. You can also drop files onto the page. See docs/songs.md.

2 players (LINK)

“Two windows” links two tabs or windows in the same browser, as if they were two FM-1s. The messages are the same MIDI messages that will go over USB-C (see docs/link.md). With two MIDI keyboards, each window can have its own.
