A rhythm game firmware for the M-VAVE FM-1.

⚠ Not yet tried on a real FM-1. VaveHero is built and tested on every change, in CI and in the FM-1 emulator, but it has not been tried on an actual device. Install it at your own risk.

Testers wanted. Have an FM-1, and know how to get it back (below the Install button)? Be the first to try VaveHero on one, and tell us how it went in an issue on GitHub: what worked, what did not, and what the screen showed.

The notes come down a neon highway, one lane for each white key, and you play them on the FM-1's own keys while its LEDs show what is coming. A synthwave band plays along: bass, pads, arpeggios and drums, every sound synthesised on the FM-1 itself. Play a phrase without a slip and the six operators fill up; press FX for OP BOOST.

An early version. Four songs for now, and more will come. Song submissions are very welcome: make one in your DAW as an ordinary MIDI file, try it in the browser version, and send it in (how).

8lanes, one for each white key
4songs, each at its own level, EASY to HARD
29patches and drum kits, all synthesised
60frames a second at most; hits judged by the sound

Install

  1. Open this page in Chrome or Edge on a computer (they can talk to the FM-1 over USB MIDI).
  2. Connect the FM-1 directly to the computer with a USB data cable, and switch it on.
  3. Close other apps that use MIDI, tick the box, press Install and allow MIDI when the browser asks.

Log

  

  
Do not unplug the cable while it writes (about a minute). If an install is cut off, the FM-1
    stays in update mode: replug it and press Install again, and it finishes. If the FM-1 does not start at all, hold
    OCT− while switching it on: it starts in the USB rescue, where an installer always works. To go back to
    the official firmware, use M-VAVE's own updater M-UPGRADE. Installing firmware is at your own risk.


  
Playing it

  

    
Title screenPLAY goes to the songs. FX plays the DEMO: the game playing itself.

    
SELECTPicks the song. Each song has its own level, EASY, MED, HARD or EXP, fixed as on the
      arcade machines and counted from how many notes come how fast: you pick the song, and the song is the challenge. The last row is MIDI MODE.

    
KNOB 2–4Your voice (TONE), the speed for practice, and the mix of you and the band.

    
ALGORITHMGRIT: how dirty the sound is, from clean to pumping and crushed.

    
GLOSPREAD: the lanes on every other white key, for big fingers.

    
SEQAUTO: the song plays itself (its result is not kept).

    
In a songThe white keys play the lanes. OCT− and OCT+ follow the octave shifts, FX sets off
      OP BOOST, PLAY pauses and HOME quits.

    
ResultsThe best result for each song is kept. PLAY plays it again, HOME goes back.

    
MIDI MODEThe FM-1 becomes the band's sound module for a DAW: one part on each channel, the keys
      sent out on channel 1.

  

  

    
    
    
    
  


  
Play it in the browser

  
No FM-1? The game also runs in the browser, with the FM-1's panel on the screen (its keys, buttons and knobs, which
    you can click, drag or touch) and the computer's keyboard or a MIDI keyboard as its keys. It is the reference the firmware is measured against: the same rules, songs and sound.
    You can load your own songs into it as MIDI files.

Your songs in VaveHero

  
A song is an ordinary MIDI file from a DAW. Channel 1 is the part you play; channels 2–6 are the band (bass,
    pad, arpeggio, lead, keys) and 10 the drums, and the audio engine plays it all in synthwave style, with several
    patches for each part. Its level is counted from its notes over time: how many notes a second, over the whole song
    and in its busiest four bars (a marker LEVEL EASY, MED, HARD or
    EXP in the file sets it yourself). Load the file into the browser version: you get a report and can
    play it right away. How it works, with templates:
    making your own songs.

  
Then send it in: open an issue on GitHub with the
    MIDI file attached, or a pull request that adds it to songs/. Say who made it and that it may be
